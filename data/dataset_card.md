**Source Link:** Primary data based on Kwizera Decor business operations (anonymized). Website: https://www.kwizerdecor.com/
**Licence:** Educational Use Only (Anonymized)
**Format:** CSV
**Size:** 502 records
**Date Collected:** November 2026
**What one record represents:** One single material or furniture item ordered within a specific customer order.

## Data Quality Report
- **Missing Dates:** 12 records have missing Order_Date values.
- **Duplicate Rows:** 4 duplicate material entries were found.
- **Spelling Variations:** The district "Kicukiro" is spelled 3 different ways (Kicukiro, Kicukiro , Kicu).
- **Formatting Errors:** Prices are stored as text strings instead of numbers in some rows.
